## About this map

Each marker is a building in the Texas A&M University Libraries' photographs of campus, placed at its original
location. The building and demolition dates come from those item records, so the map shows a building only for the
years it stood. Buildings with no recorded date always appear.

The historic imagery comes from these campus maps and aerial photographs, georeferenced with
[Allmaps](https://allmaps.org) so they line up with today's streets:

- 1914 — Map of the campus of the A&M College of Texas
- 1933 — Campus plan
- About 1950 — Campus view
- 1969 — Texas A&M University, College Station, main campus
- About 1980 — Aerial infrared image of College Station
- 1995 — NAPP aerial photograph of College Station
- About 1996 — Aerial photograph of College Station
